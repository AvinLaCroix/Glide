    If your sugar is low

    A common approach is 15 g of fast-acting carbs: 3 to 4 glucose tablets, or about 4 oz (half a cup) of juice or regular soda. Recheck in 15 minutes and repeat if still low. Skip chocolate, nuts and cheese for a low, since fat slows how fast sugar works. Follow your own care plan.

      For my care team

      Summary for my care team

      Uses the period picked on Home.

      Live Dexcom

      Connects to your own Nightscout site and adds new readings about every minute while the app is open. It works in the installed app. The claude.ai preview blocks outside connections.

      Not a medical device. Never dose from this screen. Readings can be late or missing, so keep using your Dexcom app and its alarms. Your address and token stay on this device and are left out of backups.

      Import from Dexcom

      Export your readings from Dexcom Clarity (Export, then pick dates) and choose the CSV file here. It is read on your device and never uploaded. Imported readings count toward your time in range but stay out of History.

      Settings

      Phone notifications

      Sent to your phone even when Glide is closed. Ones marked with a dot need Live Dexcom connected.

      Keep using your Dexcom alarms for lows and highs. They're more reliable for that.

      Reminders

        Alerts only ring while the app is open. For alerts when it's closed, also set an alarm on your phone.

        My meal times

        Set these to when you actually eat and sleep. They decide how your day is grouped in Patterns.

        Uses your breakfast, lunch and dinner times, only while the app is open. For alerts when it's closed, use the phone alarm times. Your care team sets your timing and dose.

        Units and target range

        The common range is 70–180 mg/dL (3.9–10.0 mmol/L). Ask your care team what is right for you. This app only logs and summarizes. It does not calculate insulin doses or replace medical advice. For severe symptoms, follow your care plan or call emergency services.

        Backup and restore

        Your data lives only in this browser. Make a backup, save it in a notes app, and paste it back here to restore it (including on a new phone).
